# Maryland Incorporated Municipalities by Jurisdiction — Research

**Date:** 2026-09-27
**Scope:** All incorporated municipalities (city / town / village, as officially chartered) in each of Maryland's 24 jurisdictions. Unincorporated census-designated places (CDPs) are excluded from the tables; short "Notable unincorporated" lines are provided separately where useful.

**Sources:**
- Primary: [List of municipalities in Maryland — Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_municipalities_in_Maryland) (featured list; revision 1337551994, last edited 2026-02-10; data attributed therein to U.S. Census Bureau and Maryland Municipal League, *Maryland's 157: The Incorporated Cities and Towns*)
- CDP context: [List of census-designated places in Maryland — Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_census-designated_places_in_Maryland) (notable unincorporated lines only)

**Notes:**
- Maryland has 157 incorporated municipalities total (per source). Four of them straddle two counties, so they appear in both county tables below: **Millington** (Kent + Queen Anne's), **Mount Airy** (Carroll + Frederick), **Queen Anne** (Queen Anne's + Talbot), **Templeville** (Caroline + Queen Anne's).
- **Baltimore City** is an independent city and its own jurisdiction (county-equivalent). It is a single entity; its neighborhoods are not listed.
- **Howard County and Baltimore County have zero incorporated municipalities** — all their population centers are unincorporated CDPs.
- Slug convention: lowercase, hyphenated municipality name (apostrophes dropped, "St." → "st-"). Slugs are unique within their county scope.
